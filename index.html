Seven days. A place to begin.
Your first Bible journey

Get to know God.
One day at a time.

You can bring your questions, your worries, and your ordinary life. Start here, just as you are.

7 days   /   about 5 minutes a day

You do not need all the answers to begin.

New to the Bible or prayer? Start here.

Find the reading: John 1:38 means the book of John, chapter 1, verse 38. Use the contents page to find the book. Large numbers are chapters; small numbers are verses. John and 1 John are different books.

Take your time: Say, “God, help me get to know you.” Read slowly, twice if you can. Think about the question, then try the prayer in your own words. Your answers can stay private. You do not need to feel something special. If you miss a day, simply continue.

About the readings: The short explanations below are summaries, not Bible quotations. Tap “Open the Bible reading” to read the verses in the Good News Translation, or find them in your own Bible.

Questions? You can ask.

If you do not understand a reading, are unsure how to pray, or have more questions, you can see Father Blaise. You are welcome to ask.

One day. One quiet moment.

Open today’s panel, read, reflect, and pray. Tick the box when you finish. Your next reading becomes available on the following day, using your device’s date. Completed days stay available to revisit. Missing a day is okay. Simply continue when you can.

You are invited

Begin: “God, help me get to know you.”

READ / John 1:38 to 39

Two people begin following Jesus. He speaks to them and invites them to come and spend time with him.

Open the Bible reading
Passage summary above. Read the verses using the link or your own Bible.

Understand

Their journey begins with curiosity and an invitation. You can begin by being curious too. You do not need to know everything about Jesus before you start getting to know him.

A moment to think

If I could ask Jesus one honest question, what would it be?

Let’s pray

Jesus, I do not know much about you yet. Help me get to know you. This is one question I have…

One small step

Spend one quiet minute speaking to Jesus about your question.

God loves you

Begin: “God, help me get to know you.”

READ / John 3:16 to 17

God loves the world and sends his Son so that people may have life through him. Jesus comes to save.

Open the Bible reading
Passage summary above. Read the verses using the link or your own Bible.

Understand

To save means to rescue. God takes the first step towards us in love. You can approach him even when you feel unworthy. Let that be the starting point of your prayer today.

A moment to think

What makes it difficult for me to believe that God loves me?

Let’s pray

God, sometimes I feel that I am not good enough for you. Help me believe that your love includes me.

One small step

When you put yourself down, pause and remember: “I am someone God loves.”

Begin: “God, help me get to know you.”

Jesus teaches that prayer does not depend on using many words. Your Father knows what you need before you ask.

Open the Bible reading

Understand

A moment to think

What would I tell God about today if I were not afraid of saying it wrongly?

Let’s pray

God, today I feel… One thing on my mind is… Please help me with…

You can begin again

Begin: “God, help me get to know you.”

READ / 1 John 1:9

When we confess our sins, God is faithful and just. He forgives and cleanses us.

Open the Bible reading
Passage summary above. Read the verses using the link or your own Bible.

Understand

To confess means to admit the truth about something you have done wrong. You can be honest with God and trust his forgiveness. Receiving forgiveness also means being willing to change and put things right where possible.

A moment to think

Is there something I need to admit and ask for help to change?

Let’s pray

God, I am sorry for… Thank you for hearing me. Help me take one good step forward.

One small step

Choose one step: apologise, tell the truth, repair some harm, or ask a trusted adult for help.

Your reflections can stay private. There is no answer to submit.

Begin: “God, help me get to know you.”

Jesus compares himself to a vine and his followers to its branches. He asks them to remain in him.
